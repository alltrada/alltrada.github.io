About Us

Alltrada is the trading brand of Drechsler Trading & Commerce, a Germany-based trade and distribution company focused on branded consumer goods.

Our business activities cover the full commercial value chain, from professional B2B procurement to distribution across established sales channels within Germany and Europe.

We source products exclusively from authorized wholesalers and distributors. All goods are genuine, properly documented, and handled in accordance with applicable regulatory and commercial requirements.

Our supplier network is built on long-term, professional relationships with reliable partners. We operate with clear contractual structures, transparent processes, and a strong focus on compliance and operational integrity.

As an independent trading company, we maintain structured internal processes and adhere to professional standards throughout our entire distribution chain, ensuring reliability and consistency in all business activities.

Returns & Refurbishment

Selected Returns. Professional Refurbishment. Individual Resale.

Alongside new goods, alltrada operates a dedicated returns business. We acquire customer returns by the pallet from established retailers and specialized returns traders, refurbish the devices with specialized service partners and resell them individually through the alltrada store on eBay.

Our focus is on household and floor care appliances from established brands.

Guarantee

Our Quality Promise

Our refurbished devices come with our own seller guarantee. The guarantor is alltrada from Dresden.

  • 12-Month GuaranteeStarting on the day the device is delivered to you or collected by you.
  • Defects After DeliveryIf a function fails, it is covered, even if the fault only arises after delivery. If the device has a battery, it is included.

Procurement

Alltrada's sourcing activities follow a structured procurement framework defined by specific product categories, clearly established exclusion criteria, and standardized operational conditions.

Product Focus Areas

We source branded consumer goods in the following categories:

  • Consumer ElectronicsSmall electronics, household devices, accessories
  • Household & HomeCleaning products, home essentials, daily use items
  • Office SuppliesStationery, paper goods, office materials
  • Personal Care & BeautyHygiene, body care, cosmetic products
  • Pet CarePet food, accessories, care products
  • Automotive, Tools & AccessoriesCar accessories, tools, DIY equipment

All products must be new, branded, and from authorized sources.

Exclusion Criteria

To maintain quality and compliance standards, we do not purchase:

  • Used, refurbished, or returned goods
  • Products from unverified or grey market sources
  • Counterfeit or unauthorized parallel imports
  • Damaged packaging or expired products
  • Unbranded or white-label items without proper documentation

  • Regular reorders for successful products
